Hey, I'm Zero.

Game developer • Music maker • Animator • Professional idea-haver

Welcome to my little corner of the internet.

I'm Zero, and I make a bunch of different stuff. I make games, music, animations, and pretty much anything else that sounds interesting enough to spend way too much time on.

A lot of my work revolves around Roblox, where I make games both by myself and with other people. I'm also the founder of TwistedPixels, a group where we work together on bigger projects.

But games aren't the only thing I do. I make music when I feel like making something that doesn't need a health bar, and I make animations because apparently making things move around wasn't enough work already.

Basically, if I get an idea and think "that would be cool," there's a pretty good chance I'll eventually try to make it.
